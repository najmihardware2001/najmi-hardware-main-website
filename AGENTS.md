# Najmi catalogue listing workflow

The owner expects a complete product listing workflow whenever products or product photos are supplied. These are continuing project preferences, not one-off instructions.

- Inspect the supplied photos and messages before choosing the product structure. Multiple colours or sizes of the same physical product belong to one parent listing with selectable variants; do not leave a colour assortment as the only selectable option when individual colours are visible and confirmed.
- Identify visible colours, construction and supplied specifications. Match them to existing listings before creating anything new. Ask one concise clarification when product identity, grouping, colour, material or measurement is genuinely ambiguous. Do not infer hidden stock, price, ratings or certification.
- Include complete English and Arabic A+ copy and imagery as part of listing products. The normal shared image set is a clean white-background studio image, material detail and a relevant use-case illustration. Use supplied references to preserve the actual product's shape, texture, material and colours.
- For colour variants, prepare individual colour images and connect each selector to the matching image and quotation item. Keep shared detail/use-case images where appropriate. Reuse the existing colour swatches and persistent mobile preview; keep the picker open after choosing a colour.
- Use exactly one small Star Rope corner watermark at the top left of each product image. Register images with baked-in branding so the website does not overlay another watermark.
- Where image approval is requested, show concrete previews before publication. Once approved, import optimized website assets, update galleries, A+ sections, colour mappings, alt text and image sitemaps, push to GitHub, follow Cloudflare deployment and verify the live result. A successful Git push alone is not proof that the change is live.
- Preserve existing catalogue identities, prices, measurement combinations, backend connections and quote/checkout gates. Unknown prices remain quote-only until actual pricing and stock are approved.
